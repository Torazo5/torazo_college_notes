# Time dilation

> [!note] Partly written by Claudian
> Your notes only had "A is moving so A's clock runs slower", so the definition above is filled in. Your original is kept below. Check it against the lecture.

a clock moving at speed $V$ relative to you runs slow: if it ticks off proper time $\Delta\tau$ in its own frame, you measure a longer interval $\Delta t = \frac{\Delta\tau}{\sqrt{1-\frac{V^2}{c^2}}}$. Equivalently, while your time $\Delta t$ passes, the moving clock advances only $\Delta t\sqrt{1-\frac{V^2}{c^2}}$.
