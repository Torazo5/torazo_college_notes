# Saturated compound

saturated compounds dont have rings and only contains single bonds.

if a saturated compounds is a hydrocarbon:
$$
C_{n}H_{2n+2}
$$

## Appears in
- [[Reading 4 - Sep 14]] — first defined here, as the baseline for degree of unsaturation
- [[Lecture 8 - Sep 17]] — the $C_{n}H_{2n+2}$ formula recapped to handle $C_{5}H_{11}Br$
