# IR active

the compression wave creates an electric wave here but this is because its H-F so that we can see an oscillating electric field — this is what we call IR active.

simply said the wave emits and that creates light itself. they also absorb IR light thus appear on the spectra

more polar will absorb stronger IR signals

same element bonds are only IR inactive if they are in identical chemical systems. In some cases theres a pretty electronegative thing and pulls electrons from one of the atoms and causes it to be slightly IR active
