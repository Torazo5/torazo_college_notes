# Spin state

protons or H-1 has two spin states $\alpha$ and $\beta$ — $\alpha$ is the same direction as the external magnetic field while $\beta$ is opposite

## Appears in
- [[Lecture 6 - Sep 14]] — introduced as the "higher energy antiparallel orientation" an H-1 flips into after absorbing a photon
- [[Lecture 7- Sep 15]] — named as $\alpha$/$\beta$, with $\Delta \epsilon_{p} = \frac{h\gamma_{H}}{2\pi} B_{p}$ for the gap between them
- [[Lecture 8 - Sep 17]] — the four combinations for two neighbouring protons, which is what causes signal splitting
