# Normal vector

$\vec{n}=(A,B,C)$ is the **normal vector**, perpendicular to the plane. The coefficients of $X,Y,Z$ ARE the normal vector.

## Appears in
- [[Lecture 4 - Sep 9]]: first defined here, in the equation of a plane
- [[Lecture 5 - Sep 14]]: $x+y+z=c$ keeps the same normal as $c$ changes, so it makes parallel planes
