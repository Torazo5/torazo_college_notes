# High performance liquid chromatography

> [!note] Partly written by Claudian
> Your note had the two phases but very tersely, so the version above fills in what each one actually is. Your original is kept below — check the completion against the lecture slide.

| Phase | What it is |
|---|---|
| **stationary phase** | tightly packed small solid particles (silica with nonpolar chains bonded onto it) |
| **mobile phase** | a liquid, typically a methanol/water mixture — the polar one of the two phases |

Because the stationary phase is the nonpolar one, this is a [[Reverse phase|reverse phase]] setup and the elution order flips versus [[Gas chromatography|GC]]: the **nonpolar** compound is retained longer, the polar one comes off first. You reason about it by asking which compound interacts with the **mobile** phase more.

To decrease retention time, make the mobile phase more polar (increase the water fraction) so the analyte prefers it.
