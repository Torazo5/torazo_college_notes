# Leading clocks lag

> [!note] Partly written by Claudian
> Your notes had the formula and the A/B example but not a general statement, so the definition above is filled in. Your original is kept below. Check it against the Sep 11 lecture.

two clocks that are synchronised in their own rest frame are *not* synchronised in a frame where they move: the clock in front (leading, in the direction of motion) reads an earlier time than the one behind, by

$$
\Delta t = \frac{D_{0}V}{c^2}
$$

where $D_{0}$ is their proper separation and $V$ their speed.
