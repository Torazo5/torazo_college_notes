# Retention time

time taken for an analyte to travel from injection to detection.

| Affinity | Retention |
|---|---|
| high affinity to the mobile phase | short retention |
| high affinity to stationary phase | long retention |

retention time is pretty variable so we have a better dimensionless, normalised measure of a solute → [[Retention factor]]
