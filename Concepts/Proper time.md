# Proper time

> [!note] Partly written by Claudian
> Your definition was one line, so the version above fills it in. Your original is kept below. Check it against the Sep 18 lecture.

the time between two events measured by a clock that is present at both events (so in that frame both events happen at the same place). It's the shortest time interval any observer measures between those events. Every other frame sees it dilated by $\frac{1}{\sqrt{1-\frac{V^2}{c^2}}}$.
