# Shielding

surrounding electron density quite literally shields the proton — more density means it feels less of the external field

## Appears in
- [[Lecture 7- Sep 15]] — derived from diamagnetism: the induced opposing field makes $B_{p}$ less than the external field
- [[Lecture 8 - Sep 17]] — tied to the ppm axis, where less shielding means higher ppm
- [[mid term revision]] — quick review chain: pulled electrons → less shielding → more field → higher ppm
