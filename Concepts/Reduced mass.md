# Reduced mass

> [!note] Partly written by Claudian
> Your definition was just "mass of the atoms", which isn't enough to compute with, so the version above fills it in. Your original is kept below — check the completion against the Lecture 4 slide.

the effective mass of a two-atom vibrating system, combining both atomic masses into one number:
$$
\mu = \frac{m_{1}m_{2}}{m_{1}+m_{2}}
$$
It is always smaller than either individual mass, and it's dominated by the *lighter* atom — which is why swapping H for a heavier atom changes the vibration frequency a lot. Along with the force constant, it's one of the two things that set where a bond absorbs in IR.
