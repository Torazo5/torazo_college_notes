# Electron domain

non bonding loose pair, single double or triple bonds — pretty straight forward just where electrons are kinda grouped

A double bond, a single bond, or a lone electron pair — each counts as one

## Appears in
- [[Reading 3 - Sep 9]] — first defined here, as the thing VSEPR counts
- [[Lecture 5 - Sep 10]] — used for geometry and whether bond dipoles cancel
- [[quiz prac]] — 4 domains gives a tetrahedral shape; 4 domains with 1 lone pair and 3 bonds is trigonal pyramid
