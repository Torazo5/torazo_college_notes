## Summary

The rhino problem as a timing picture: the two blade clocks read $0$ and $-4.8/c$ because of leading clocks lag. Each blade drops when its own clock hits $t=0$, so the front one drops right away and the rear one has to wait out its $4.8/c$ lag. That waiting time is dilated from the observer's frame, and the blade's frame gives the proper time because that's where the drop happens.
