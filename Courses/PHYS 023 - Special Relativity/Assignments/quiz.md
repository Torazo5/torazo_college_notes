Yeah. The cluster of stuff you’ve been doing is basically **relativistic energy and momentum**, especially **particle collisions/decays**.

The best label to put in your notes is:

**Special Relativity: Energy, Momentum, and Particle Decays**

Under that, the main subtopics are:

- **Relativistic energy**
    
    \[ E=\gamma mc^2 \]
- **Relativistic momentum**
    
    \[ \vec p=\gamma m\vec v \]
- **Energy-momentum relation**
    
    \[ E^2=p^2c^2+m^2c^4 \]
- **Photons / massless particles**
    
    \[ E=pc \]
- **Conservation of energy and momentum in relativistic collisions and decays**
    
- **Lorentz transformation of energy and momentum**
    
    \[ E'=\gamma(E-Vp_x) \]
    
    and the corresponding momentum equations
    
- **Velocity transformations between frames**
    
- **Four-momentum / momentum four-vector**
    
    \[ \left(\frac Ec,\vec p\right) \]

The specific thing you were struggling with in the muon problem is usually called:

\[ \boxed{\text{relativistic decay kinematics}} \]

or more broadly:

\[ \boxed{\text{relativistic collision and decay problems}} \]

That’s the part where you repeatedly do:

\[ \text{initial energy}=\text{final energy} \]

and

\[ \text{initial momentum}=\text{final momentum}. \]
