## Summary

A short lecture that picks up from the reading. Orbitals are again a distribution of where electrons could be. Because of diffraction and interference, things behave as both particles and waves, so electrons get described with shapes that look like standing waves. Squaring that wave gives the PDF of the electrons, which is what an orbital actually shows.
