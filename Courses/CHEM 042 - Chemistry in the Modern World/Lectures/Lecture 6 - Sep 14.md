## Summary

A short intro to proton NMR: each H-1 nucleus spins randomly and produces its own small magnetic field, its magnetic moment. Applying a strong external magnetic field organizes those, and then feeding in a specific radiowave frequency makes some H-1 absorb photons and flip into the higher-energy antiparallel orientation. Reading out the result gives the spectrum, where each distinct kind of hydrogen shows up as its own unique signal. This is the setup that [[Lecture 7- Sep 15]] then makes quantitative with the $\alpha$/$\beta$ spin states and the energy gap.
